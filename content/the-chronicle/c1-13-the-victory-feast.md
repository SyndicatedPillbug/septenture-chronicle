<span class="dateline">Oxia 20th, 1396cr · Mayor Greyson's manor, Lobridge</span>

# 1.13 — The Victory Feast

Two days after the siege, Lobridge still smelled of smoke in the low streets, and the town threw a feast anyway. The mayor's manor filled with music and wine and people who had spent a night holding a wall. The Five came cleaned up and dressed in their best, and none of them trusted the evening.

Legion stood by a window and watched the room the way they watched everything. Coy had not dressed for a party and could not have said why he was there if asked; the bear left marks that take time to fade, and he had spent the last week feeling like something with teeth at his back. Page spent the first hour with Morwenna, speaking the old language with the mayor's wife, and found a sadness in her that had nothing to do with the battle. She said she had not had cause to use elvish in a long time.

Simon found Lord Blackwood, a winemaker with a trade agreement in Latesa and a memory of Simon's mother. Blackwood asked after the Bohuns, and mentioned that he had not heard from them in some time. Simon filed that away with the rest of the things he had been told lately.

Ceridwen joined the bard Eldragh for a song they both knew, the second oldest in Septenture. Halfway through, the melody took her somewhere else. Coy went with her, and the room went grey around them while a pulse of light beat from the place where the bone bracelet had touched them both. Coy came back first and went to her before he thought to ask about himself. Ceridwen missed a beat, and Eldragh covered it like a professional while she found her hands again.

Then Legion felt the heat signatures coming at a run, and went out the window rather than toward the door. They had been saying all evening that the peace was wrong, and they were right.

The manor's front doors blew in on a spell. A brute of a woman with a short blade looked over the crowd, put her finger on Simon, and shouted that he was the one. Ceridwen put a witch bolt into a swordsman bearing down on the mayor; his chainmail and his own steel did the rest, and he went spinning across the floor like something struck by lightning. Page set his drink on a table and said that this was rude, and put his rapier up under the brute's ribs from behind. Coy answered the men at the back with a swarm of everything the forest keeps quiet about, and knocked the assassin off Simon.

The fight went on longer than the opening. Legion stopped a mage's mouth with silence and took him out of the evening entirely. Simon answered a knife with dragon's breath and a hand full of lightning. Coy went into his lion shape and helped Page carry a bomb out of a burning room before it could take the manor down.

They took one of the attackers alive, and he talked before morning. Baron Lewis Zollern had paid for the night, for an old grievance against Simon's family. Morwenna came to them after, and let them see what she was: an elf, older than the town, with more years on her than she had let anyone guess. The ring that Fiona had stolen belonged to her goddess. She asked them to bring it back from Latesa.

She also warned them about what lay under that city, and spoke to each of the Five alone before she let them go. Whatever she told them, they all came out of the room quieter than they went in.

They left Lobridge with their gear repaired, a mule named Bill, and a road south.
