# 18 — Official Business

Nara opened the door when she saw the blood-soaked jacket. She knew the cloth and the torn seams. Ceridwen's face did the rest. Nara spoke in a whisper. Watch informants stood on every street in the Basin. Neighbors who asked questions had vanished. Someone had threatened her.

Then men came into the alley with cudgels and leaded billies, and warrants that were not the Watch's. They called Selim a traitor and swung for his skull. Selim held the mouth of the alley while the others got room to work.

Magic cracked the street open. The spellcasters showed what they were. Legion's wasps filled the air until the alley hummed. When it was over the attackers were down, Nara was alive, and the party had a prisoner who was afraid of his own answers. One man got away, which meant somebody would eventually come looking for the rest.

They took their prisoner to the Hive's old sanctum and asked him what he had been sent to do. The men were city militia, on official work, sent to break up a meeting of Libertine terrorists. Under that story sat money, and Vardon's name. One of the dead men carried a necromantic twist in him. Something had touched him and left a mark.

Simon's murder, the missing poor, the frightened witnesses, and the Watch's silence all ran back to House Vardon. The corrupt side of the Watch would be looking for them. Their one lawful ally was Captain Stross, and his protection ended at the district gates.

And in the corner of the room, a prisoner sat very still, knowing just enough to be frightened of his own answers.
