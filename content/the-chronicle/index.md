# The Chronicle

The campaign in the order the party lived it, with stories from the days between sessions. Each chapter carries its date in the world. Where the table skipped a week, the numbers skip with it.

## Part III — Latesa

*Umbria, 1396cr. The party reaches the city in time to watch the shipyards burn.*

- **[5 — The Bohun Fire](c3-05-the-bohun-fire)** — the party reaches Latesa in time to watch a shipyard burn.
- **[6 — The Slow Carriage](c3-06-the-slow-carriage)** — the fire's last room, a performance for the guards, and a carriage that left too calmly.
- **[7 — Bill](c3-07-bill)** — a mule goes missing, a butcher gives him back, and the bracelet takes a shot at Page.
- **[9 — The Shrouded in the Alley](c3-09-the-shrouded-in-the-alley)** — an alley fight that becomes a queen's throne room.
- **[10 — The Bohun Estate](c3-10-the-bohun-estate)** — shelter behind a family's walls, and a first conversation with one of the dead in the bracelet.
- **[11 — The Bohun Breakfast](c3-11-the-bohun-breakfast)** — Page wakes with his memory out of true, and Legion finds kin in the garden.
- **[12 — Stross](c3-12-stross)** — Simon meets the captain and learns how much of the murder case was never written down.
- **[13 — The Loss of Simon](c3-13-the-loss-of-simon)** — a captain arrives at the estate with news the party understands before he speaks.
- **[14 — The Cistern House](c3-14-the-cistern-house)** — the party goes to grant citizenship to wasps, and Legion meets a swarm that is afraid.
- **[15 — Who Am I](c3-15-who-am-i)** — a new mind asks its first question, and Page finds what the hive is anchored to.
- **[16 — The Witness in the Basin](c3-16-the-witness-in-the-basin)** — a seamstress with a secret, a brand on a stranger's wrist, and a dozen men in an alley.
- **[18 — Official Business](c3-18-official-business)** — the fight for the witness ends, and the men who started it turn out to be the city's own.
- **[19 — The Sanctum](c3-19-the-sanctum)** — Nara goes somewhere the Watch will not look, and the party uses the quiet to prepare.
- **[20 — The Maze](c3-20-the-maze)** — a night watch on the overgrown grounds, and a shape that melts into the hedge.
- **[21 — Ten Quiet Days](c3-21-ten-quiet-days)** — rest, practice, watch rotations, and a sound in the sluice that was not water.
- **[22 — The Brood Theft](c3-22-the-brood-theft)** — smugglers come up the sluice for Hive's brood, and Legion learns rage.
- **[23 — Something Else Is Coming](c3-23-something-else-is-coming)** — the cistern answers a call, Hive goes up through the glass, and the party runs for the sluice.
- **[24 — The Gunslinger](c3-24-the-gunslinger)** — caught between a stranger with two pistols and the militia behind them.
- **[25 — The Courtyard and the Wish](25-the-courtyard-and-the-wish)** — *Glacies 4th, 1396cr.* A patrol surrenders. The stranger fires again. Ceridwen reaches for Morgath, and the survivors wake high above the sea.
- **[The Wrong Hunter](the-wrong-hunter)** — *the night after.* Page hides under a root and sends his double out to meet the creature that has followed him through centuries of sleep.
- **[Interlude — The Stone](interlude-the-stone)** — *Glacies 5th.* The water queue takes the party deeper into Rockside. A body passes on a borrowed door; a boy arrives with a captain's token.

## Part I — Campaign One: The Feylight Festival

*Oxia, 1396cr. The search for a missing child leads through the festival gates to the ruins beneath Lobridge.*

- **[1.1 — The Feylight Festival](c1-01-the-feylight-festival)** — a missing boy, a weasel with a grudge, and the gates of the festival.
- **[1.2 — The Clockwork Stall](c1-02-the-clockwork-stall)** — a fortune, a broken globe, and a missing boy's debt.
- **[1.3 — The Hag in the Tent](c1-03-the-hag-in-the-tent)** — the hag dies, and the festival's proprietor comes calling.
- **[1.4 — Moonfire Stew](c1-04-moonfire-stew)** — a night of shopping, a menagerie, and pixies whose key has gone missing.
- **[1.5 — Wit and Words](c1-05-wit-and-words)** — under the well, a trickster sets three games. Ceridwen answers the last one with a song.
- **[1.6 — Dark Dreams and Propositions](c1-06-dark-dreams-and-propositions)** — the bracelet takes a wrist, and a stranger is waiting at breakfast with a contract.
- **[1.7 — The Cellar Door](c1-07-the-cellar-door)** — the mayor gives them his cellar, and Fiona gives them the truth.
- **[1.8 — If You Ain't Wight](c1-08-if-you-aint-wight)** — a riddle in a locked door, and the dead behind it.
- **[1.9 — The Helm and the Ring](c1-09-the-helm-and-the-ring)** — the poem opens the tomb. Fiona takes the ring, and Page follows her through a teleport.
- **[1.10 — Bad Omens](c1-10-bad-omens)** — a rider named Edreth, a farm full of the dead, and a message for a man named Corvain.
- **[1.11 — Before the Storm](c1-11-before-the-storm)** — a town arms itself in an evening, and two riders come into Lobridge at a gallop.
- **[1.12 — The Siege of Lobridge](c1-12-the-siege-of-lobridge)** — the Shrouded come over the river. Ceridwen takes one of them, and the bracelet pays a debt.
- **[1.13 — The Victory Feast](c1-13-the-victory-feast)** — two days after the siege, Lobridge throws a feast, and the assassins come with the wine.
