## Part I — Campaign One: The Feylight Festival

*Oxia, 1396cr. The search for a missing child leads through the festival gates to the ruins beneath Lobridge.*

- **[1.1 — The Feylight Festival](c1-01-the-feylight-festival)** — a missing boy, a weasel with a grudge, and the gates of the festival.
- **[1.2 — The Clockwork Stall](c1-02-the-clockwork-stall)** — a fortune, a broken globe, and a missing boy's debt.
- **[1.3 — The Hag in the Tent](c1-03-the-hag-in-the-tent)** — the hag dies, and the festival's proprietor comes calling.
- **[1.4 — Moonfire Stew](c1-04-moonfire-stew)** — a night of shopping, a menagerie, and pixies whose key has gone missing.
- **[1.5 — Wit and Words](c1-05-wit-and-words)** — under the well, a trickster sets three games. Ceridwen answers the last one with a song.
- **[1.6 — Dark Dreams and Propositions](c1-06-dark-dreams-and-propositions)** — the bracelet takes a wrist, and a stranger is waiting at breakfast with a contract.
- **[1.7 — The Cellar Door](c1-07-the-cellar-door)** — the mayor gives them his cellar, and Fiona gives them the truth.
- **[1.8 — If You Ain't Wight](c1-08-if-you-aint-wight)** — a riddle in a locked door, and the dead behind it.
- **[1.9 — The Helm and the Ring](c1-09-the-helm-and-the-ring)** — the poem opens the tomb. Fiona takes the ring, and Page follows her through a teleport.
- **[1.10 — Bad Omens](c1-10-bad-omens)** — a rider named Edreth, a farm full of the dead, and a message for a man named Corvain.
- **[1.11 — Before the Storm](c1-11-before-the-storm)** — a town arms itself in an evening, and two riders come into Lobridge at a gallop.
- **[1.12 — The Siege of Lobridge](c1-12-the-siege-of-lobridge)** — the Shrouded come over the river. Ceridwen takes one of them, and the bracelet pays a debt.
