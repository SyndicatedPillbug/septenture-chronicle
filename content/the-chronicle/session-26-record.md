# 26 — At the Hollow Heart

After the courtyard escape, the companions found shelter at the Hollow Heart. They recovered while the city closed around them: soldiers took the junctions, the ox elevators stopped carrying ordinary traffic, and the inn filled with people who could no longer get home.

## A city under orders

The bells announced a military mobilization larger than Page expected the Vardons to command. He estimated twenty to thirty thousand soldiers. Watch patrols began entering Rockside, where they had not traditionally policed, while the militia restricted movement through the city.

By the third day, wanted posters made travelling together dangerous. The drawings were imperfect but recognisable. Page’s ears were drawn accurately. Coy’s notice described a druid and shapeshifter, including animal forms; Legion was described as a burn victim. The masked gunslinger was wanted in connection with the deaths of important nobles and a watchman.

Page saw a Bohun ship enter through the harbour blockade and signs of arcane activity at the Wizards College. What the College was doing remained unclear.

## Page

Page helped the party reach the Hollow Heart after the courtyard escape and paid for their rooms. During their recovery, Coy helped support him and coaxed him away from the window. Later, Page helped disguise Coy as a healer associated with a local order.

Page told Ceridwen that he had concealed his identity for years, was centuries old, and had watched over generations of the Devereaux family. He said he had known Simon from birth and had promised to protect him.

## Ceridwen

Ceridwen used Sending to contact Stross without disclosing the party’s hiding place. Stross warned that his precinct was surrounded, records were being taken, and officers were being replaced.

During the questioning of the masked newcomer, Ceridwen cast Zone of Truth. The newcomer resisted the spell. It therefore gave the party no magical confirmation of her account.

Ceridwen later told Page that she trusted and loved him, but needed more time before speaking fully about what she was experiencing.

## Coy

Coy examined the newcomer’s severe scarring with help from the party. He judged it to be established scar tissue and was wary of attempting an ordinary surgical repair.

He wanted to work as a healer among people harmed by the lockdown and look for information about Haven. Page helped with his disguise.

## Legion

Legion described sealing the inn’s door with clay at night, supplying goodberries, and sending animal messengers to look for Selim and the other hive. The messengers detected Hive. A reunion did not occur during this session, and the search did not establish a new location for Selim.

## The gunslinger’s account

The newcomer introduced herself as **Sandra Vale**, said she was an agent of the Devereaux family, and said she was investigating Simon’s killers. Page suspected she might be using an alias.

She removed her wide-brimmed hat inside the inn but kept her mask on. The recording describes dark red hair, longer on top, with a severe undercut. She said she needed the mask to survive and showed the party severe scarring that extended beneath its edge. Coy examined the injury.

## Stross and the northern captain

Stross reported that his precinct was surrounded, his records were being taken, and officers were being replaced. He urged the companions to find shelter and stay together. Ceridwen’s Sending had not disclosed where they were hiding.

He arranged a meeting with a friend at a bread stall along one of Rockside’s yellow lines. Page, Ceridwen, and the gunslinger went in disguise. Their contact was a northern sea captain in a fur cloak and chainmail, with a sword bound into its sheath with human hair.

The captain contacted Page telepathically. One of the people rescued from the Bohun shipping-yard fire was her son. She had married into the Bohun family and considered the companions allies. She offered passage if they needed it and planned to follow them back for a fuller conversation. Page bought sourdough, giving the visit an ordinary appearance.

## The proclamation

A proclamation carrying Victor Vardon’s seal named several supposed terrorist groups, including the Fire Starters. Victor announced a celebration honouring his son, who would rule the city during Victor’s unexplained absence. The companions continued to suspect Cedric and Lewis in Simon’s death.

The winter solstice and Ceridwen’s birthday, on the 16th, were approaching. Coy intended to help the injured in Rockside and look for leads concerning Haven. Page urged everyone to travel in at least pairs and identified an old hiding place as a possible fallback. Coy’s investigation had not yet played out in this session.

## An interrupted conversation

Page spoke to Ceridwen about Simon, the promise he had made to protect him, and the grief he carried after failing to keep that promise. Ceridwen told him that she trusted and loved him, but needed more time before confronting everything she was feeling. When she referred to the power at her wrist, Page offered to help if she could tell him more.

For Ceridwen, Page’s voice became muffled. Morgath appeared beside her, apparently unseen by everyone else, and told her to finish the conversation quickly: they needed to talk. He vanished. The session ended there.

## Still unresolved

The captain’s offer had not yet become an escape plan. Legion’s messengers had detected Hive, but there had been no reunion, and Selim’s location remained unknown. Coy’s search for Haven, the gunslinger’s stated identity, the College’s magic, and Morgath’s urgent conversation were all left open.
