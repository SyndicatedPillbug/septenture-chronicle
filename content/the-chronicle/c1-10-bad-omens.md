<span class="dateline">Oxia 18th, 1396cr · the road south of Lobridge</span>

# 1.10 — Bad Omens

Page tracked them for an hour before the heat-shimmer ahead dissolved and Fiona came into view with twenty armed men and no interest in stopping. He followed them up the hill and watched them meet a larger force, and then split: the ones with horses went south, and the ones on foot turned north, toward Lobridge. He could feel his books. They were with the horses.

He sent Fiona a message through the air, because a man who has just been robbed has opinions to share. No matter how far you run, I will hunt you down. Give me back my books. She answered that she was sorry, that she had not had a choice, and rode on. Page turned and went after the walking men instead, as mist, at a distance, on foot.

The rest of the Five were at the Quill and Quillow, where the man on the table was dying in a way Coy had seen before. Coy helped Elara make a narcotic to hold him, and the two of them bought him hours instead of minutes. Legion went scouting, and took a tree for privacy, and came out of it in their true form: a swarm, moving over the southern roads at speed.

The trail the injured man had walked was five miles long. It ended at a farmhouse where more than ten bodies had been torn apart and stacked inside, guild medallions among them, a day dead. The animals outside were untouched and unhappy about the disruption to their routine. Legion spoke with the pigs first, on the grounds that pigs tell the truth, and got the shape of it: people who should not have been there, then fear, then worse people, then more fear and more death. The cows added the detail that mattered. Two of the killers had not been men at all. They had been predators.

Simon was left watching the dying man while Elara worked. About halfway through, the man's eyes opened and his hand closed on Simon's coat with the strength that sometimes arrives just before the end. They're coming. He said it again, and then said they had only been meant to follow, but people were being torn apart and they had to help. He made Simon promise to tell Corvain they had failed. It took Simon a moment, after, to place the face. Another survivor of Coalmyr Gate.

He left Elara to finish the ritual and went to the mayor's house with the news. Magistrate Caspar Degnr drew a sword on him first and grudgingly sheathed it second. Simon talked past him, told Mayor Greyson what had happened at the Quill and Quillow, and asked for the town's defenses to be raised that night. Greyson believed him. Degnr was ordered to prepare the town.

At the same hour, Page was riding behind a stranger who had been tracking the marching men. The man's name was Edreth, his horse was called Fennel, and he did not care for the guild. Page gave his name as Luerilan and his politics as Libertine, which warmed the conversation considerably. Edreth explained what the marching soldiers were. The Shrouded, servants of powerful magic users, and hard to kill. They were four hours out of Lobridge. Page said he would fight with him, and fell asleep in the saddle.

Then Elara found him with her spell, and the two of them passed messages he was too tired to remember afterward. He told her the town was in danger. She told him his friends would know he was coming. Edreth rode faster.
