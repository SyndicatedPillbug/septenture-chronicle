*Oxia 17th–18th, 1396cr · the ruins, the road, and the Quill and Quillow*



Barbello's poem had four lines and no manners, and Page and Ceridwen set to taking it apart in the inner sanctum. Beneath old stars, where whispers dare to spread. A name bound in shadowed dread. Rain inverted in an idol's helm, and a king where waning moonlight hides.

A mirror hung above one alcove, catching the helm's reflection. Light came down from a shaft in the ceiling and made the mirror more than decoration. Legion reached for the helm and their hand went through it. Ceridwen saw the crescent moon etched on its brow. Simon threw his cloak over the shaft, and with the room dark except for the helm, the thing turned solid enough for a hand to hold. Page tried to lift it and it refused him.

Then the names on the tombs. Simon found one he knew: Rexos, an old Solnokom word for king, and the name of the first king of Alderbris. Page stood in Rexos's alcove and looked at the reflection properly. The vase held water. Coy lifted the water out of the vase with druidic magic and set it inside the helm, and the helm came down to be taken.

While every eye was on Rexos's tomb, Fiona had found what she came for. A panel stood open in the wall behind her and the ring was in her hand, and she looked at Coy and Ceridwen like a woman apologizing to someone with no time to hear it. Then the smoke bomb went off.

She was out of the sanctum before the coughing stopped. Legion went after her with the wasps. Page built a telepathic link between himself, Legion, and Coy, and Coy nearly answered it as Mander, from the life he had before Latesa. Page ran next, faster than a librarian ought to run, and caught her in the mayor's own cellar at the edge of a circle she had already drawn.

He lunged. The magic took them anyway. Page held on and went through with her.

They landed in a room with ten armed men in it. Fiona said she was doing it for the greater good, which Page did not accept in any form, and while he told her exactly what he thought of it he kept the link open and fed his friends everything he saw. Then a hand came down on the back of his head, and the link went out.

In the cellar, Coy felt the silence where his friend had been. Legion tested the spell's leavings with a wasp; the wasp did not come back. Simon read the magic as something familiar and out of his reach. They climbed out of the ruins to find Mayor Greyson back with the magistrate and Ernest Copperfield, sealed the site with Ernest's help, and told them the short version: wights handled, Fiona gone, Page gone with her.

Carly Two-Ton sent them to the Quill and Quillow, where a woman named Elara Nightshade was healing a man who had walked in off the road bleeding. Coy helped with the man and saw that his wounds came from something like a wight. Elara agreed to try to find Page, and needed pieces of him to do it. They gave her a book he had left, notes shaken loose from its pages, a sketch Ceridwen had made of his face, and his canteen.

Some hours later, far from any of that, Page woke alone in an old watch house with his head splitting and half his belongings gone. Anything elvish, anything fey, anything worth carrying had been taken, including books. A coin lay on the floor with a shark on it, familiar in a way he could not place through the fog. He walked out into the grass, found tracks leading toward a road, and started walking after the people who had his things.
