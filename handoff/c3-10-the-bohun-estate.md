*Umbria 17th–18th, 1396cr · the Bohun estate, the Whites*



They kept Page breathing through the streets and carried him up to the Whites, and there the Bohuns took them in.

Roseem's household took charge immediately. A physician examined Page before the introductions finished, servants brought clean linens and warm broth, and the heavy doors were barred against the street. Simon's relatives sheltered the party without hesitation, treating the aftermath of the riot as a family crisis to be handled behind stone walls. The Five slept beneath a secure roof for the first time since leaving the coast.

Every hour under that roof tied Simon tighter to a family that expected its obligations paid. The guild, the Watch, and the magistrates stayed too dangerous to trust.

That night, Ceridwen deliberately reached into the power of the bone bracelet. Agnes Cantar answered from the dark: another trapped soul the hags had bound into the relic. Agnes spoke plainly. The bracelet was a prison designed to siphon and store human souls, holding stolen power that could be channeled if Ceridwen learned to endure the strain.

Morning came with the smell of breakfast. The household was up, dressed, and quiet. Page was alive, unconscious, under a doctor's care, and would stay that way a while. Ceridwen had a real lead on the thing on her wrist and no idea what to do with it. Coy carried two problems for the walk ahead: the healer's worry, and the face of Windhaven Plonnet, a dead friend, walking the streets of a living city.

Simon weighed whether to go meet Captain Stross, the family's safest lawful contact, and what it would cost to be seen doing it.

Legion, who had felt something the night before and spent the morning hunting the feeling, found the answer in the gardens. A rival colony of their kind, close by, and awake.
