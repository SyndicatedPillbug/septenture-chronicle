# 25 — The Courtyard and the Wish

<!-- source: content/the-chronicle/25-the-courtyard-and-the-wish.md | dateline: Glacies 4th, 1396cr · the courtyard outside the Whites | description: A Vardon patrol surrenders in the courtyard outside the Whites. The stranger fires again, and Ceridwen accepts Morgath's help to end the fight. -->

*Glacies 4th, 1396cr · the courtyard outside the Whites*



Three war-hounds came through the fog, with Sergeant Rell Hask close behind. At the alley mouth, the black-coated militia held their line. Ceridwen pushed herself up from the star-brick paving where Coy had healed her, fixed her eyes on the sergeant, and let a dolorous bell toll inside his skull. She hexed his strength, too. Then she smiled at him.

One of the hounds took Page by the leg and pulled him down. He lost his hold on the gale that had pinned the patrol, and the soldiers began to rise.

Selim met the sergeant with his sword, struck again, and found enough strength for another rush. Legion spread grey mist across the alley mouth and waited at its edge. Coy fought in bear form while the spectral hounds he had called tore at the swordsman and halberdier. When Ceridwen whispered into the halberdier's ear, he fled bleeding down the alley. The Blade and Hask looked at what remained of their patrol. They dropped their weapons and opened their hands.

The stranger stepped out of the fog and fired into the Blade. A second shot struck Hask. Both men had surrendered; both were still alive when she lowered her gun.

Ceridwen reached for the power at her wrist.

She had felt its cold before, and heard the voices inside it. Now Morgath offered his help, and she accepted. Warmth rose through her arm. She spoke a wish, and red light spread from her across the courtyard. Soldiers fell where they stood. It reached her companions, too.

Coy fought it. For a moment his own magic held a little space around his body, and through it he saw a tall man at Ceridwen's shoulder: long black hair braided past a high collar, a gaunt face, clothes centuries out of fashion. Morgath rested a hand on her with a familiarity Coy would remember after he woke. Across the paving, gold light encased Page from head to foot.

Then Coy's resistance gave, and the dark took him.

---

They woke on a cliff.

Moonlight caught the water far below the ledge. Cold wind moved through their wet clothes, and the lamps of Latesa marked the streets beneath them. Coy had returned to human form. The gunslinger was there. Selim was gone.

Their last clear memory was the courtyard. They had left two people behind the dry fountain, people they had promised to protect. The militia lay somewhere below, along with an unopened dispatch case and whatever Selim had done after the wish took hold. The halberdier had escaped.

Page had stayed conscious. Alleviatira's touch had held him up through the red light and the journey to the ledge, long enough to see Morgath for himself. Now his strength had gone. He sat against the rock until the others began to stir, watching a lamplit window partway down the cliff face.

He knew where they could sleep. With the others awake, he got them moving toward that window and the rooms at the Hollow Heart. He paid, lay down in his boots, and slept for eight hours.

The dream found him there.
