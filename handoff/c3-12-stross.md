*Umbria 18th, 1396cr · the Watch precinct, the Shave*



While the others prepared to move on the cistern house, Simon went alone to keep his appointment with Captain Jebezer Stross, and found a man more desperate than the party had guessed.

Stross laid out two problems and made it clear they were one problem. The first was the murders: young women killed in the Shave and the Basin, in ways the city had learned to file under Sickle work and forget. The second was the vanishings — hundreds of names over years, no case structure, no bodies, no paperwork that anyone would own. The Watch had failed at both, and the Guild's patrols had failed at both, and Stross no longer believed the failure was incompetence.

The details made it worse. Only one of the murdered women had ever reached a proper examination before the body was removed; the rest had been cleaned away. Witnesses kept disappearing. Precinct evidence kept disappearing. All six of the dead women had passed through Costella's Music Hall in the weeks before they died, which is the sort of pattern that survives a careless investigation only when someone is protecting it.

He gave Simon copies of the case notes and a live witness to find: a woman named Lamatt Skritav. He asked for help and did not pretend the request was safe.

Back at the estate, Page and Ceridwen finally talked about the wound. Page refused to put it on her, and said plainly that the bracelet was the thing at fault. Ceridwen listened, and did not agree, and did not let herself off either. The conversation ended better than it started, which is not the same as ended.

Simon left the precinct with the notes under his coat and three men waiting for him in the alley before he had finished his follow-up questions.
