*Umbria 18th, 1396cr · the Watch precinct, the Shave*



While the others prepared to move on the cistern house, Simon went alone to keep his appointment with Captain Jebezer Stross, and found a man more desperate than the party had guessed.

Stross laid out two problems and made it clear they were one problem. The first was the murders: young women killed in the Shave and the Basin, in ways the city had learned to file under Sickle work and forget. The second was the vanishings — hundreds of names over years, no case structure, no bodies, no paperwork that anyone would own. The Watch had failed at both, and the Guild's patrols had failed at both, and Stross no longer believed the failure was incompetence.

Stross showed Simon the precinct logbooks. Only one of the murdered victims had received an autopsy before an armed detachment transferred the body out of Watch custody; the other five had been removed from the morgue within hours of discovery. Informants had gone missing, and case files showed razored gaps where depositions had been removed. All six victims had worked or drunk at Costella's Music Hall in the days before they died. Someone with access to the precinct had been cutting the case down as it was written.

He gave Simon copies of the case notes and a live witness to find: a woman named Lamatt Skritav. He asked for help, warning that anyone seen looking into the records would become a target.

Back at the estate, Page and Ceridwen spoke alone about the necrotic blast that had wounded him. Page said he did not blame her. He blamed the bracelet. Ceridwen nodded. She did not look relieved.

Simon left the precinct with the notes under his coat and three men waiting for him in the alley before he had finished his follow-up questions.
