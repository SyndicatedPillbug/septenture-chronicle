# 1.11 — Before the Storm

<!-- source: content/the-chronicle/c1-11-before-the-storm.md | dateline: Oxia 18th, 1396cr · Lobridge, evening | description: A town arms itself in an evening, and two riders come into Lobridge at a gallop. -->

*Oxia 18th, 1396cr · Lobridge, evening*



Nobody in Lobridge slept early that night. The Five had an evening to turn a market town into something that could survive a raid, and they spent it moving.

The plan came together in the mayor's front room, mostly without the mayor. Wilherd Greyson heard the first part of it and then took a horse and went looking for his wife, who was due back from her travels and did not come home on time. Legion recruited scouts from the men still sober enough to ride, and put a ranger in the woods who knew his work. Simon woke Coy and Ceridwen and gave them the short version. Carly Two-Ton, who had been feeding this party for days and had a good eye for what a building is for, agreed that the Temple was the only structure worth defending, and started moving the town toward it.

Coy led the walk as a rainbow horse, which is not a sentence anyone planned to say. A parade of nervous townsfolk followed him through the streets, most of them children, with Ceridwen walking behind to gather stragglers. The militia pulled what weapons they had out of what chests they were in. Degnr gave his orders, disliking every one of them.

Out on the Old Road, one of the scouts came back with the news that mattered: a body of men had split, some crossing the river at a low spot and others disappearing into the trees to the east. That is the shape of an encirclement, and Legion said so before anyone else did.

Edreth and Page reached the town at a gallop, passing the marching column on the way, close enough for Page to count them. A rider came at the town gate fast enough to scare the sentries, and Ceridwen stood in the road with her feet planted because she had told the militia she would stop a horse if she had to.

The horse skidded to a halt in the dust. Page dismounted, and he and Ceridwen caught each other's hands in the middle of the street, relieved to find each other alive. Then they walked to the green together.

Degnr recognized Edreth on sight and reached for his sword, and Page put himself between two men with long histories and told them the enemy was close enough to hear the argument. Edreth kept his mouth shut. The Five took the last of their positions, Coy went back to the Temple as a man again, and the town waited.

The first footfalls came up the road as full dark settled. Then a lot of them.
