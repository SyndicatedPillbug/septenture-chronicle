# 21 — Ten Quiet Days

*1396cr · the Whites and the cistern*



The city let them have the time, which none of them entirely trusted.

They took it in shifts. The watch at the cistern ran in rotations, two at a time, walking the sluice line and the tunnel mouths and the hedge with the widened gap in it. Nothing came up the sluice in the dark, night after night. The caltrops in the human entrance went untouched and kept their arithmetic quietly. Hive learned the sound of each of them walking, and started calling them by the shapes of their footsteps.

The rest of the days went to the work the city had been teaching them. Coy learned to put his teeth into a thing without putting his whole soul behind it. Page learned new uses for the wind that had been living in him since the courtyard, and practiced them where nobody official could watch a librarian do it. Ceridwen worked at the bracelet with Lilliana's instruction in her ear, and hated some of what she learned and used it anyway. Legion practiced being Legion with more of Legion in it. Simon was at the Bohun estate most of those days, in a family conversation that never quite finished, doing useful things with an engineer's patience that nobody thanked him for until later.

Nara sewed. She mended half the party's clothes in the sanctum and said it kept her hands quiet, and what she overheard of the hive learning words, she did not say a word about to anyone.

On the ninth night the watch heard something in the sluice that was not water.

It was small, and slow, and patient, and it stopped when the wind stopped, and started again when the wind did, and by the end of the night it had gone back down the tunnel without showing itself. The watch reported it as a rat, or a current, or a stone settling.

The tenth night, it brought friends.
